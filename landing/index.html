Frequently Asked Questions

Is my patient data stored on your servers?
No. MedScribe AI processes text in real-time and does not persist any clinical data. Input and output are discarded after each request.
What AI model powers MedScribe?
MedScribe uses a fine-tuned Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.2 model, trained on 10,000+ medical Q&A pairs using QLoRA (4-bit quantization). It achieves +18.4% ROUGE-L improvement over the base model.
Can I integrate MedScribe into my EHR system?
Yes. Professional and Clinic plans include full REST API access with OpenAPI documentation. POST your clinical text, get structured SOAP notes back as JSON.
What happens if I exceed my note limit?
You'll receive a notification when approaching your limit. Upgrade to Professional (unlimited) anytime — your existing notes and data remain intact.
Is there a free trial?
Yes! Every new account gets 20 free SOAP notes to evaluate the quality before committing to a paid plan.
Can I self-host MedScribe?
Enterprise customers can deploy MedScribe on their own GPU infrastructure using our Docker images. Contact us for details.
